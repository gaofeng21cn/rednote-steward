Generation route: built-in imagegen tool. The successful generation used an
opaque white paper background, matching the logo references.

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: GitHub repository logo and small application icon, square high-resolution raster, no text.
Primary request: Redraw the RedNote Steward logo in the same hand-illustrated brand family as the provided logos and the newly illustrated product overview.
Input images: Image 1 (RedCube AI logo) and Image 2 (OPL Relay logo) are visual-family references for the segmented circular motif, color palette, dark ink outlines, paper texture and pencil shading. Image 3 (RedNote Steward overview) is a texture/style reference. Image 4 (old RedNote Steward logo) is a semantic reference ONLY: content cards with a photo and a verification check. The old image's flat SVG appearance must be replaced by naturally illustrated ink and colored pencil. These are references for a new image, not a collage.
Subject: one bold, compact, text-free emblem for local publishing and post management. A slightly tilted red content card over one off-white rear card. The red card has a simple white-framed landscape thumbnail (teal mountain, blue sky, one tiny golden sun) and two short caption strokes. A clear teal verification check in a small off-white circular badge overlaps the lower-right edge of the content card. Keep the main card and check large and visually distinct at 64–132 pixels.
Brand-family composition: surround the central content-card symbol with a thick, naturally hand-inked three-part circular orbit: dark navy at upper left, teal at upper right, brighter blue across the lower arc, separated by two deliberate gaps near the top and lower-left. Add the family’s small golden circular node with navy center on the right side of the orbit. Follow the reference logos' visual balance and topology. The content-card symbol is the unique project identity. Avoid crowding it with extra icons.
Style/medium: thick nearly-black hand-drawn ink contours with subtle natural width variation; rich colored-pencil fill and tactile fine hatching; white notebook-paper rounded-square tile with faint widely spaced light-blue dots; gently organic edges and professional polish. Rounded square with about 8% breathing room around the emblem. Small paper-edge depth is fine, but keep the symbol crisp and high contrast. It should feel like the same illustrator made the reference logos and overview.
Palette: deep navy, teal and brighter blue for the orbit; warm red for the central note; a small golden-yellow accent node; black ink and off-white paper. Use the reference palette.
Background: clean white outside the subtly rounded-square white notebook-paper tile, as in the RedCube AI logo reference. Preserve the square icon composition and light paper texture.
Text: none. No letters, initials, product name, labels or watermarks.
Constraints: create one finished square icon, not a sheet of variants or a mockup. Match the illustrated texture of the references; keep silhouette and the central check easy to identify when small. Do not imitate Xiaohongshu's official logo. Do not include a medical cross, envelope, cube, stethoscope, extra badges, photo-realistic objects, vector-perfect uniform strokes, gradients or glossy 3D effects.
```
